How to play

  • Pick a board from the presets or set your own under Custom board. X always moves first, and the first to get k in a row (across, down or diagonally) wins. With exactly k, a line longer than k does not count.
  • Click an empty cell to place a stone; on big boards stones go on the grid intersections, as in gomoku. With gravity (Connect Four), click anywhere in a column and the stone drops to the lowest free cell.
  • Undo takes back moves to your previous turn. Swap sides exchanges the X and O players and starts a new game.
  • Set both players to agents to watch them play each other. Pause and Step let you go one move at a time. To run many games at once, use the Arena.
  • The address bar keeps the board and players, so you can copy the link to share a setup.

Agents run in a background thread, so the page stays responsive while they think. The Last agent move panel shows what the agent did: nodes searched, playouts, MENACE's matchbox, or why it refused a board.
